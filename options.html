Sieve works in your browser with your own key.

Start here

Sieve needs one key before it can score anything. It takes about two minutes.

  1. Get an OpenRouter key at openrouter.ai/keys and add a few dollars of credit. Scoring costs about 3 to 5 US cents per 1,000 posts.
  2. Paste it into OpenRouter key below and press Check and save keys.
  3. Open LinkedIn, X, Reddit or YouTube. Posts get a small Sieve tag as you scroll.

Keys

Scoring

Stored only in this browser. Each key is checked before it's saved. Scoring runs on Jev, TypeSafe's decision model, reached through your OpenRouter key: about 3 to 5 US cents per 1,000 posts. Sieve is independent and not made by TypeSafe.

A TypeSafe key is only used when no OpenRouter key is saved: then Sieve scores with Jev directly at TypeSafe. Briefs, digests and Watch it for me still need an OpenRouter key.
